## Manifestation lifecycle first: E8 and E9

### E8, what gets forgotten: **L8a passes for shaping by eye by the letter, but nothing is forgotten; the natural control improves more**
| Rule | Extra fall at width 4 (≥ 0.10) | Treatment − filtered (expected within 0.03) | L8b ratio 4 vs 64 (≥ 2) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wear | 0.094 [0.075, 0.115]: neither | 0.030: within | 1.11: fail |
| Shaping by eye κ = 1 | 0.114 [0.092, 0.138]: **pass** | 0.036: between 0.03 and 0.05 | 1.30: fail |

- **Every arm's edge accuracy rises during T2.** At width 4: treatment +0.04 to +0.06, filtered +0.07 to +0.09, natural +0.15. The "extra fall" is the gap in improvement, not a loss.
- **Why:** the preregistered plateau rule (change < 0.01 between successive 2,000-step checks) stops pretraining at a median of 11k–16k steps (minimum 4k), at edge accuracy 0.70–0.80. Wild agents in E10 reach 0.85–0.89 by 30k–60k. So agents enter T2 still learning, and the built world mostly withholds the edge items they would have kept learning from. The pilots reported the same ("mostly the natural control improving").
- **What this does and doesn't show:** building stops edge learning (treatment gains about a third of what the natural control gains), and it does so about as much as the matched filtered control (within 0.03–0.04), which fits "building matters as the cause of the shift". It doesn't show ordinary forgetting of something learned. L8b fails: the gap barely depends on width.
- **Matching checks pass:** offered edge shares within 0.45 points of each other; acted turns within 1%.
- **Population vs individual (figure data in `summary_E8_traj.csv`):** the built share stays at 0.95–0.99 throughout T2 while individual edge accuracy sits at 0.69–0.76.

## What surprised me
1. **E8:** no forgetting in any arm, because the plateau rule stops pretraining early. The criterion passes for shaping by eye on a difference in learning, not in retention.
2. **L9b fails while L9 passes.** The log-loss jump at the snap is smaller than pre-registered (0.06–0.09 nats), even though the payoff gap is large.
3. **ε_r for the passive matched arm didn't transfer** from tuning seeds to real seeds (1.18 × → 1.47 ×), so that arm can't be binned.
4. The ensemble manipulation ratio is far above pilot 4's (3.6). Mean-of-ratio values are inflated by small centre spreads; the ratio of means is 3.7–6.2.
